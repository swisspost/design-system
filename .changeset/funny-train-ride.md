Added a `show-home-text` property to `<post-breadcrumbs>` to display `text-home` as visible text instead of the home icon, enabling segment specific root links (e.g. "Private customers"). Also added a required `text-expand-home` property, used as the accessible label for the overflow menu shown when the home item no longer fits.
