### Routing with Client-Side Frameworks

To enable client-side routing (e.g. Next.js, Angular Router), slot your own anchor instead of using the `url` property. The component detects the slotted `<a>` and stops rendering its own, leaving your link's click handling untouched.

<post-banner type="info">
  <p>
    The slotted `<a>` must be a direct child of `<post-language-menu-item>`, not wrapped in another
    element, otherwise it won't be detected and the component will fall back to rendering its own
    internal link or button.
  </p>
  <p>
    The `url` property is ignored once you slot your own `<a>`. Since link attributes are no longer
    applied automatically, give the slotted `<a>` an accessible name and add any framework-specific
    routing attributes directly to it.
  </p>
</post-banner>
