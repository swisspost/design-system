- **Minimal and to the point.** Toasts are small interruptions, so keep their content short and non-interactive.
- **About two lines of text.** If the message needs more, show it on the page instead.
- **No links or buttons.** If users need to act on the message, place the action on the page or use a dialog.
- **The type of message is clear from the text,** not only from its color and icon.
