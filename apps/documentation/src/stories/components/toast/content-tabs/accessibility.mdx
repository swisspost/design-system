## Live region

- Toasts are intended to be small interruptions to your visitors or users, so to help those with screen readers and similar assistive technologies, you should wrap your toasts in a live region.
- Render the live region before adding toasts to it.

See how to implement it on our [live regions documentation](/?path=/docs/548d973c-679c-4a02-96cd-70f82aec5f65--docs).
