<span slot="header" className="d-flex align-items-center gap-16"><post-icon name="document" className="w-32 h-32 flex-shrink-0" aria-hidden="true"></post-icon><span>Content<span className="d-block fs-6 fw-normal">Up to 4 levels · Short labels · Icons on level 1 only</span></span></span>

- **Up to four nested levels.** Keep the hierarchy as shallow as possible: more than three levels are hard to navigate.
- **Short, descriptive labels** that tell users where each item leads.
- **The section heading is optional.**
- **Icons only on first-level items,** on all of them or none, and only icons that clearly relate to their label.

</post-accordion-item>

- **Consistent item types.** Expandable elements are either always `post-collapsible` elements, or never, in which case they are always expanded.
- **Parent items only open their section.** An item that opens a section does not also navigate to a page.
