## Routing with client-side frameworks

By default, `<post-breadcrumb-item>` renders its own `<a>` inside the shadow DOM based on the `url` prop. Frameworks with client-side routing (e.g. Next.js, Angular Router) can't intercept clicks on that internal anchor, so navigating triggers a full page reload.

To keep client-side routing working, slot your own anchor (or the framework's `<Link>` component, which renders as an `<a>`) instead of setting `url`. `<post-breadcrumb-item>` detects the slotted `<a>` and stops rendering its own anchor, leaving your link's click handling untouched.
