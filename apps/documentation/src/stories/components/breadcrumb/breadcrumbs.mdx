### Routing with Client-Side Frameworks

To enable client-side routing (e.g. Next.js, Angular Router), slot your own anchor instead of using the `url`/`home-url` properties: use the default slot for `<post-breadcrumb-item>`, or the `home` slot for the root item of `<post-breadcrumbs>`. Both components detect the slotted `<a>` and stop rendering their own, leaving your link's click handling untouched.

<post-banner type="info">
  <p>
    The slotted `<a>` must be a direct child of `<post-breadcrumb-item>` (or of `<post-breadcrumbs>`
    for the `home` slot), not wrapped in another element, otherwise it won't be detected and the
    component will fall back to rendering its own internal link.
  </p>
  <p>
    The `url`/`home-url` properties are ignored once you slot your own `<a>`. Since the label,
    description, and `aria-current` are no longer applied automatically, give the slotted `<a>`
    an accessible name (via its text content or `aria-label`), and add `aria-current="page"`
    yourself on the item that represents the current page.
  </p>
  <p>
    The `selected` property still works as expected: it applies the correct visual styling and
    controls the item's position in the breadcrumb trail.
  </p>
</post-banner>
