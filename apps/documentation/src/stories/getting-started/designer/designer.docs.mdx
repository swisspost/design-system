## Which Figma seat suits me?

Choose a seat based on how you need to work with Figma.

### Figma «Full Seat»

For designers, product managers and team members who actively create designs, prototypes and
collaborate within the team.

### Figma «Dev Seat»

For developers working on the implementation of designs in code. Inspect designs and access the
specifications needed to build them.

### Figma «View Seat»

For users who only need to view and give feedback on existing design projects, without editing or
creating content.

### Figma «Guest Seat»

For guests who need temporary access to a project. Guests must be activated by an administrator.
